# Your first letter

This short lesson writes a letter with **Veranda Write**, Veranda's
word processor. It takes about ten minutes. You learn to start a
program, type and style text, save, close and open a document.

## 1. Start Veranda Write

1. In the desktop, click **Apps** in the window's sidebar.
2. Double-click **Veranda Write**.

An empty page called "Untitled" opens. The cursor is at the top.

## 2. Type the letter

Type a few lines. Press **Return** only at the end of a paragraph: the
text wraps by itself at the end of a line.

    Dear Ada,

    The garden is in bloom. Please come and see it on Sunday.

    Best wishes,
    Marta

To put today's date at the top: move the cursor to the start (Ctrl+Home
or Ctrl+PgUp), choose **Text > Insert date and time** (Ctrl+Shift+D),
and press Return. The date comes from the X16's clock, so set the clock
first (see [Date, time and time zone](Date-and-time.md)).

## 3. Add a heading

1. Click in the first line.
2. Choose **Style > Heading 1**. The line becomes large and bold.
3. When you press Return after a heading, the next paragraph is Body
   text again.

## 4. Make a word bold

1. Double-click the word "Sunday" to select it.
2. Press **Ctrl+B**, or click **B** in the toolbar.

**Ctrl+I** makes text italic and **Ctrl+U** underlines it. **Ctrl+T**
makes it plain again. With nothing selected, these keys set the style
of what you type next.

## 5. Save the letter

1. Press **Ctrl+S** (File > Save). The Save box opens in
   `/DOCUMENTS`.
2. Type a name, for example `Letter to Ada`. Long names are fine.
3. Press **Return**. Veranda Write adds `.cwr` to the name.

The title band now shows the name. A `*` after it means there are
changes that are not saved.

## 6. Close and open it again

1. Choose **File > Close** (Ctrl+W). If there are unsaved changes,
   Veranda Write asks "Save the changes ...?".
2. Choose **File > Quit** (Ctrl+Q) to go back to the desktop.
3. Click **Documents** in the sidebar. Double-click **Letter to Ada.cwr**.
   Veranda Write starts with your letter.

## 7. Put it aside

Press **Alt+Tab**. Veranda Write goes to the dock with your letter,
and the desktop comes back. Click its icon in the dock to come back to
the letter, exactly where you were.

## What next

- [Veranda Write](Veranda-Write.md): all of its features and keys.
- To print the letter, choose **File > Export as PDF**, copy the PDF
  to a PC and print it there.
- **File > New from template...** has a ready-made letter, CV, report
  and meeting notes.
