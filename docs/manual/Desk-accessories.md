# Desk accessories

A desk accessory is a small program that opens **over** the program you
are using. When you close it, the program under it comes back exactly
as it was.

| Accessory | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Calculator](Calculator.md) | a scientific calculator |
| [Note Pad](Note-Pad.md) | pages of short notes |
| [Dex](Dex.md) | an address book |
| [Calendar](Calendar.md) | a month, appointments and alarms |
| [Preferences](Preferences.md) | Veranda's settings |
| [Photo Manager and Text Manager](Photo-and-Text-Manager.md) | albums of clipboard pictures and texts |

## How to open one

- **In the desktop:** choose **Veranda > Desk accessory**, then pick one
  from the list.
- **In a Veranda program:** the first menu (the program's name) has
  **Desk accessory...**.
- **Double-click** it in `/VERANDA/APPS` (the desktop comes back when
  it closes).
- Preferences also opens from the menu bar's clock and speaker.

The list shows the desk accessories in `/VERANDA/APPS`.

## How to close one

Click its close box (top left or top right of its window) or press
**Esc**.

## Memory

A desk accessory needs some free RAM banks. If there are none, close a
program you put aside (right-click it in the dock, **Quit**).
