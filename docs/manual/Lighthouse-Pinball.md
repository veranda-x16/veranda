# Lighthouse Pinball

Lighthouse Pinball is an original pinball table: a harbour at night,
with a lighthouse, a ferry ramp and fog banks, in full colour. Version
1.0. MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 Marcin Spoczynski.

Lighthouse Pinball is not built into every package. If it is not in
`/VERANDA/APPS`, copy `Lighthouse Pinball.cvt` there.

## Start it

Double-click **Lighthouse Pinball** in `/VERANDA/APPS`. Press **Enter**
to start a game.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Z or left Shift | the left flipper |
| / or right Shift | the right flipper |
| Space or Down (hold, then let go) | the plunger: hold longer for a harder launch |
| Left, Right, Up | nudge the table (too much: TILT) |
| Enter | a new game |

The mouse is only for the menus. Opening a menu pauses the game.

| Menu | Items |
|---|---|
| Lighthouse | About Lighthouse Pinball |
| Game | New game, High scores, Quit |
| Options | Sound: on/off |

## How to play

- **3 balls** a game. A ball that drains in the first 10 seconds after
  the launch is saved.
- **Three missions** (25,000 each):
  - **BEAM**: light all four lanes at the top. The flippers move the lit
    lanes. Each time: 10,000, and the bonus multiplier goes up (up to
    5X).
  - **Buoys**: knock down all three drop targets: 10,000.
  - **Ferry**: ride the ferry ramp three times (5,000 a ride). A ride
    within 4 seconds of the last is a **combo**: it counts twice and
    scores 15,000 more.
- **Jackpot**: with all three missions done, the lighthouse is lit. Hit
  the lantern for 50,000 times the level. The first jackpot of a game
  gives an **extra ball**.
- Other points: fog bumpers, slingshots, the harbour loop and its
  spinner, the inlanes and outlanes.
- **Bonus** at the drain: lanes, buoys and ferries, times 1,000, times
  the multiplier. A tilted table scores nothing.

## High scores

The five best scores, with a name you type at game over ("Your name,
harbour master:"), and the sound setting, are saved in
`/VERANDA/.SYSTEM/Lighthouse Pinball Scores`.

**Alt+Tab** puts the game aside, and it comes back as it was.
