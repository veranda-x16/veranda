Tomatoes on a Balcony

By Ada Gardener, 21 November 2026

Growing tomatoes on a small balcony is easier than it looks. A sunny wall, a deep pot and a little patience are all you need; the plants do the rest.

Choose a cherry variety for a first year: they ripen early, forgive a missed watering and keep cropping until the first frost. See also the seed library.

What you need

  • A pot of at least 20 litres
  • Compost & a little grit
  • A cane and some soft string

“The best fertiliser is the gardener’s shadow.”

A watering table

Month   Litres/day
June    1.5
July    2.0

Water at the base, never the leaves, and pick the fruit as soon as it colours.
Enjoy them warm from the sun.
